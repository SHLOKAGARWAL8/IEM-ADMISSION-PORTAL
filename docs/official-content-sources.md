Accuracy notes:
- Fee values are copied from the current public IEM course page as seen on 7 November 2026. Confirm with current official admission circulars before payment.
- Faculty experience durations are not fabricated. Where the public profile does not state experience or course allocations, the UI says so and links to the official faculty portal.
- Some requested fest names (Impetus, IEMPHYS, HEALS, Cognitia) did not have independently verifiable current edition details in the reviewed sources. They are shown as names requiring organiser confirmation, without invented dates or claims.
- Hostel existence (separate boys' and girls' hostel facilities) is listed on IEM's website. No availability count or current hostel fee was found; the portal directs applicants to confirm these.
- Placement outcomes are historic cohort-specific figures and explicitly not guarantees.
- The IEM and UEM logo assets are referenced from the official Dean Academics site: https://dean.iem.edu.in/assets/iem-logo-white.png and https://dean.iem.edu.in/assets/uem-logo-white.png.
